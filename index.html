Our Story

Winsford's Favourite Local Shop

Nodens Sandwich Shop has been serving the people of Winsford with freshly made food every single day. We're a local bakery and sandwich shop with a simple mission — great food, fair prices and a warm welcome.

Everything is prepared fresh each morning in our kitchen. From our breakfast batches to our handmade cakes and pastries, quality and generous portions are at the heart of everything we do.
